# Proki v0.1.4

Proki now asks less, and sounds like a friend when it does. Your apps and websites show their own
icons, and tasks, notes and automations each open in a dialog of their own.

## ✨ Highlights

- **Fewer questions.** Questions Proki asks on its own now wait for a shared quiet gap of 30
  minutes, set as `nudge_gap` in the global settings. A question you leave unanswered makes the
  next wait twice as long.
- **The right greeting.** Morning start says good morning, afternoon or evening by the clock.
  It offers time to settle in only before noon, and names your first task.
- **Kinder words.** Every routine is reworded: "You're in the zone. Want me to start a session
  and keep things quiet for you?"
- **A small win at the end.** Stopping a focus session shows how long you focused, with a
  cheering cat. The end of the workday closes with a note of your deep work, with nothing to
  answer.
- **Icons on the Apps page.** Each app and website shows its own icon, on macOS and Windows.
  The table fits the window, and a row is deleted in place.

## 🌱 Routines

| Routine | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Morning start | At most two questions a day, down from four. Mondays bring back what you said you'd change on Friday. |
| Focus sessions | "Drifting?" at most every 10 minutes, down from every minute. "Later" on the break asks again in 25 minutes, not 2. |
| Thought catcher | Asks only after 5 minutes of distraction, no longer on every email or chat window. |
| Note sorter | Sorts new notes quietly; asks right away only when one sounds urgent. |
| Busywork check | Once a day, down from every 30 minutes. |
| Session suggestions | At most once every 2 hours. |
| End of workday | No more 12-option question about when to sort notes: they wait for tomorrow. Friday's answer is kept for Monday. |
| Inbox guard | Once a session. |
| Bedtime | A reminder every 20 minutes, down from every 5. Past midnight, the alarm is unchanged. |
| Time on tasks | Checks in after a break at most once an hour. |
| Evening check-in | Removed: End of workday already asks what's still open. |

A routine you changed yourself stays as you left it. On an existing install, Evening check-in
stays too: switch it off in Automations.

## 🎛️ The app

- **Tasks** open in their own dialog, with a search box. Tags carry over when you reorder.
- **Notes**: each bucket is a table. A note opens as a dialog with Markdown, plus New and search.
- **Automations**:
  - The cards are grouped by tag.
  - A state's dialog saves its actions, transitions, name and cycle together.
  - A transition opens with a table of its conditions.
- **The graph**:
  - Add a state with a button.
  - Drag from one box to another to connect them.
  - A broom deletes the states nothing leads to.
- **Actions** share one layout: set, add, poke, ask, the LLM, Jev, open and lock.
- **Metrics**: each chart has its own time range and opens from its title.
- **Today**: slimmer focus panels, and tasks in columns.
- **New icons**: the cat on a transparent background, filling the app and tray icons.

## 🐛 Fixes

- The tray menu uses the system font, and sits under its icon near the screen's edge.
- The window opens at launch, and when Proki is started a second time.
- A greeting showed an empty line about today's work: nothing filled it in. It now shows your
  first task.
- In Tables, the last row no longer keeps a line under it.

## 🛠️ For builders

- Tests run in their own workflow, on macOS and Windows, for every push and pull request.
- macOS builds run on `macos-15`.
- With the `APPLE_*` secrets set, the macOS app is signed with a certificate of the project's
  own, so macOS keeps its permissions from one version to the next.
- MCP: `nudged_at`, `nudge_gap` and `may_nudge` are new globals, for routines of your own that
  should respect the quiet gap.

## ⚠️ Known limits

- The macOS app isn't notarized by Apple. Before opening it the first time, and after each
  update, run:

  ```bash
  xattr -cr /Applications/Proki.app
  tccutil reset All app.proki.ui
  ```

  Then open Proki and turn it on under **Accessibility** and **Input Monitoring**.
- On Windows, if SmartScreen appears, choose **More info › Run anyway**.
- There's no Linux version yet.
