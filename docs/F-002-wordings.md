# F-002 1.7: the popups' wordings, for you to read through

Each question gets 2 to 4 wordings, one picked by the clock each time it's asked (the minute it's
asked in: random enough for questions minutes or hours apart). The first of each is the one there
is now; the others are new. Its options (the buttons) don't change.

How to review: strike (~~like this~~) or delete a wording you don't want, edit one in place, or
add one. Leave a question as it is to take all of them. `{…}` is what the program fills in.

Not varied here, since they're already worked out from how sure jev is (1.1, 1.2): the app's
label, the note's bucket, "Deal with it now?" and the task picker's and planner's own questions.
